Where to check private vacancies

Company ki apni Careers ya Jobs website ko pehli preference dein. Vacancy ka title, city, qualification aur closing date employer ke official page se verify karein.

  • Nestlé Pakistan — company information and career navigation.
  • LinkedIn Jobs — employer-posted opportunities; verify the employer before applying.
  • Rozee.pk — Pakistan job listings; confirm details with the employer.

Safe application checklist

CV ko role ke mutabiq update karein. Interview ya job offer ke liye advance payment, OTP, bank PIN ya password kabhi share na karein. Offer ko company ke official contact se confirm karein.
